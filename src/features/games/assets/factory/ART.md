# Arte de Fábrica de Pedos

Generado con la herramienta integrada image_gen. Los fondos y las poses se convierten a WebP mediante scripts/prepare-factory-art.mjs. La portada se obtiene de una captura del juego en ejecución, no de generación de imágenes.

## Fondos

Archivo: backgrounds.webp. Atlas de cinco columnas y dos filas, en el mismo orden que FACTORIES.

Prompt utilizado:

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: industrial platformer game background atlas. Create ONE image sheet with exactly 10 equal rectangular panels in a strict 5 columns by 2 rows grid, no gutters, no text. Landscape sheet 2560x1536 if possible. Each panel is a photorealistic detailed straight-on orthographic cross-section of the BACK WALL of a huge factory, realistic gritty metal materials, bolts, weathered brick, pipework, ducts, cable conduits, scaffolding, catwalks IN THE BACKGROUND ONLY, dramatic volumetric industrial lighting, sharp crafted game environment, readable darker middle, no foreground platforms or hazards or characters. Distinct panels left-to-right top row: orange iron foundry with furnaces; blue-grey steam engine plant with valves; green chemical factory with glass tanks; cyan frozen cold storage with frost; amber food cannery with conveyor belts. Bottom row: purple electrical plant with coils; red petroleum refinery with oil pipes; blue robot assembly line with mechanical arms; green nuclear reactor room; violet orbital factory with windows into space. Consistent camera and scale in all 10 cells. Fill every panel edge to edge. High quality realism, not vector art. No typography or UI.

## Personaje

Archivo: hero.webp. Atlas transparente de cuatro columnas y dos filas. Se conserva el canal alfa al convertir a WebP. El gas y los obstáculos se animan en Canvas.

Prompt utilizado:

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: transparent game character sprite atlas, 4 columns by 2 rows, exactly 8 equal cells. Create a high quality realistic 3D rendered ADULT male platformer protagonist, full body, front-facing in every cell, natural human proportions and face (not chibi, not toy), short dark hair under blue baseball cap, royal blue soccer jersey with ONE broad horizontal yellow chest stripe, BLACK athletic shorts, white socks and dark sneakers. Realistic fabric weave, seams, wrinkles, skin shading and anatomy, warm studio key light with cool rim. Same identity, same scale, exact same ground baseline and centered on each cell. Full body visible with generous transparent padding and absolutely no clipping. Eight poses left to right: top row relaxed standing; walking left foot forward; walking right foot forward; bracing and squatting with effort face ready to fart. Bottom row strong fart propulsion with knees bent up and elbows back, determined cheeks; airborne with legs tucked; falling with arms spread; landing crouched. Front view only, keep cap/jersey/shorts identical. No gas effects: generated by game. Genuine transparent background, no contact shadow plane, no text, no labels, no borders, no extra objects. Production sprite atlas of 8 isolated adult characters.

## Alimentos

Archivo: foods.webp. Diez comidas realistas, en el mismo orden que FOODS. PNG fuente conservado junto a cada atlas para futuras correcciones; el juego carga solamente los WebP optimizados.

Prompt de generación:

Use case: product-mockup. Asset type: transparent sprite atlas for realistic videogame food collectibles. Exactly 10 isolated food servings in an evenly spaced grid, 5 columns by 2 rows, no text no labels no extra objects. Orthographic three-quarter front view, each centered and fully contained in its equal square cell with generous transparent padding. Consistent lighting, realistic appetizing food textures, sharp high detail. Top row left to right: slice of cheese pizza on white plate; white ceramic bowl of cooked brown beans; a red apple on white saucer; white plate of spaghetti with tomato sauce; cheeseburger on white plate. Bottom row left to right: cooked broccoli florets on white plate; peeled yellow banana on white saucer; two filled tacos on white plate; peeled boiled egg with half showing yolk on white saucer; bowl of lentil stew. Real transparent background everywhere outside the objects, preserve natural material shadows on plate itself, no floor, no colored backgrounds, no typography, no decorative props. 5 by 2 grid with no borders.

Edición final: retirar únicamente el velo blanco fuera de las siluetas y conservar posición, tamaño, alimentos y platos; exigir alfa cero en el espacio vacío y preservar el blanco opaco de los platos. Se utilizó image_gen integrado con transparent_background=true.
